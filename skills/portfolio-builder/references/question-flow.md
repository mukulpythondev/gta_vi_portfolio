# Question flow

Ask 3–5 questions per round. Offer the default in brackets so "default" is an answer. Pre-fill from any resume, LinkedIn export or GitHub profile the user shares.

## Round 1: theme

1. **Theme.** What world should the site feel like? Any answer works: a genre (cyberpunk, noir, fantasy), a culture or epic (Bollywood, Ramayan, Greek myth), a medium (retro arcade, comic book, newspaper), a hobby (cricket, F1, cooking).
2. **Which side of it?** E.g. Bollywood: 70s masala posters, 90s romance, or modern blockbuster? Ramayan: temple-mural classical, Amar Chitra Katha comic, or minimal modern? [the most recognisable one]
3. **Mood words.** Three adjectives (e.g. "grand, warm, playful"). [derived from theme]
4. **Brand colours** to keep, if any. [derive from theme]
5. **Motion level.** Calm, Story or Immersive (see SKILL.md). [Story]

## Round 2: you

6. Name, and the display name if different.
7. Role headline in your words.
8. Bio in plain words (a paragraph); optionally a technical version.
9. Location and availability. [omit]
10. Portrait photo? [initials fallback]
11. Up to three numbers you can prove (years, users, wins). [skip]

## Round 3: work

12. 3–6 projects. For each: name, hook (under 10 words), one sentence anyone understands, the hard constraint, the result with a number, top three technologies, status, demo and repo links, and whether you have a screenshot or short screen recording.
13. Experience: company, role, dates, 2–4 achievements with outcomes, stack.
14. Awards, hackathons, talks, certifications, with proof links.

## Round 4: skills

15. Skills grouped by category. Skills used in projects are counted automatically.
16. Anything you do not want shown?

## Round 5: contact

17. Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, other socials, resume PDF.
18. Main call to action. [Email me]
19. Domain. [set later]

## Round 6: extras (optional)

20. Photos for a gallery, with where and when.
21. Your story as a timeline (study, jobs, turning points).
22. An easter egg? [Immersive only]

## After the interview

Write the theme sheet (references/themes.md) and the brief (templates/brief.md). Mark every gap as missing; confirm before building.
